Abstract.

Label matchers decide which answers count as correct, and calibration metrics inherit those calls. We report 1062 paired comparisons in fifteen strata (plus 412 regime-contrast rows excluded from N): exact matching versus a semantic-tolerant matcher and its guarded revision v3.3. Descriptive study: the guarded matcher nests the exact one, so reverse flips are zero by construction; no significance claimed. Flip counts (exact-wrong/guarded-right versus reverse): pilot 11 versus 0 of 30 (descriptive pilot data, no top-up); a same-model pool 9 versus 0 of 220; a cross-domain loose-prompt layer 20 versus 0 of 60; rollups holdout 7 versus 0 of 108 and local-new 12 versus 0 of 217. A loose-regime rerun shows the prompt-regime contrast is question-type-dependent. ECE moves track the accuracy shift; no independent calibration claim. Adjudication confirms 99 of 106 rows put to judges (102 flips plus 4 probes; Methods). A one-rule guard reproduces the 120-row adjudication exactly in-sample (one fix, zero regressions); a synthetic probe measures its boundary (formula 12/12, CJK 8/8); a pre-registered loose-replication stratum contributes 24 of 26 flips; a hetero-family stratum (gemma3:4b) contributes 8 of 8. A blinded 25-item gold set grades 24 of 25 (divergence G06). A 100-item stratified extension (author-graded blindly; external-volunteer second rater agrees 29 of 30, $\kappa=0.91$) grades 90 of 100 (88 of 100 adjudicated; all 62 flip rows agree; 9 guard false negatives plus one author slip (H64)). A retest reproduces the flip set exactly. Temperature scaling and isotonic regression show no consistent ECE repair. Adjudicated guarded accuracy is 922 of 1062, reported per stratum.

Introduction

Calibration papers routinely report accuracy and expected calibration error (ECE) as if the correctness labels were fixed. When correctness comes from a programmatic matcher, the matcher is part of the measurement instrument. A brittle exact matcher can mark semantically correct answers wrong (``8848.86米'' vs ``8848'', ``不会'' vs ``否''), depressing accuracy and inflating ECE; a tolerant matcher reverses those calls and the calibration conclusion moves with them.

This manuscript reports one descriptive estimand on paired rows: D1, the flip asymmetry between exact-wrong/guarded-right (b) and exact-right/guarded-wrong (c) counts. No significance test is run on D1: the guarded matcher nests the exact matcher (Methods), so c=0 by construction on non-degenerate rows and any McNemar p-value would be a definitional tautology. Separately, two non-inferential conventions: a descriptive 5% flip-rate bar inherited from the label-matcher validation gate (G006: unadjudicated flips above 5% block any calibration claim), and a procedural gate (not part of D1): each flip must be adjudicated before any calibration claim is made (the adjudication gate).

We deliver 1062 paired comparisons in fifteen strata. The pilot subset (n=30 of the pre-specified 50; observed-status) clears the descriptive bar (b=11, c=0, descriptive only; pre-specified 50 unmet with no top-up available, so the pilot stays descriptive pilot data). The holdout-main subset (n=30) clears the descriptive bar (0.1667 exceeds 0.05) with no inferential test run, so its calibration conclusion stays hedged. The cloud-new subset clears the descriptive bar (0.0833 exceeds 0.05) as a descriptive observation (b=5, c=0 raw; sensitivity n_ceil=217 under the placeholder odds ratio (OR=4, analysis_plan_power_rep.md), disclosed). Its size is n=60 (fresh questions, qwen2.5:7b live at temperature 0). The local-new subset answers the same 60 questions on qwen2:7b at temperature 0. Its size is n=60 with the same descriptive pattern (0.0833 exceeds 0.05; b=5, c=0; sensitivity n_ceil=217). The English subset (n=30) shows the boundary condition: 0 flips, so neither the descriptive bar nor D1 is met there and no adjudication is triggered. The local-big subset (n=160, arithmetic-heavy with recomputable golds) flips 4 of 160 (0.025, b=4, c=0; sensitivity n_ceil=720). A pre-specified same-model pool of local-new plus local-big (n=220; post-hoc n_ceil=217, composition pre-specified) shows b=9, c=0 while missing the descriptive bar (0.0409); both readings are reported descriptively. Related work

Our positioning starts from published measurement practice. Exact string matching underlies standard QA scoring from \citet{rajpurkar2016squad} onward, while semantic-similarity metrics such as \citet{zhang2020bertscore} and model-based judges \citep{zheng2023judging} trade strictness for coverage; each choice moves which answers count as correct before any downstream metric is computed. Post-hoc calibration by temperature scaling \citep{guo2017calibration} and binned ECE estimation \citep{nixon2019measuring} both assume fixed correctness labels, so label-side variation of the kind we study propagates into calibration conclusions by construction. Standard background on deep learning follows \citet{goodfellow2016deep}. A contemporaneous reproducibility audit of knowledge-graph extraction from threat reports independently supports the matcher-as-instrument view: re-scoring fixed system outputs under eight matching protocols reverses 11 of 45 pairwise orderings (F1 spanning 0.16–0.70), no mechanical matcher (lexical, embedding, or entailment) agrees with multi-reviewer adjudication above 71%, while an LLM judge reaches 86% \citep{hakim2026ranked}. Its domain is security extraction rather than QA calibration, so it is cited as independent motivation, not as a comparable effect size.

Within that landscape, plus a 30-day arXiv incremental screen (374 records, 11 double hits each adjudicated with zero counterexamples), we are not aware of prior work reporting same-batch exact-vs-tolerant flip magnitudes with row-by-row adjudication of this form. The screen protocol is the repo-root scout_c07_screen.py over the 30-day arXiv increment (374 records, three queries) with the adjudicated hit table in LITERATURE_REVIEW.md; the 44-record supplementary screen is paper_workflow_kb/scripts/incr_scout_0907.py.

Two observations motivate the design. First, the pilot magnitudes themselves motivate the design: exact matching gives 63.3% accuracy and 0.363 ECE, rising toward 100% accuracy and near-zero ECE after adjudicated relabeling (previously recorded as internal card L01 on the same 30 rows). This paper's delta over L01 is the fifteen-stratum replication, the second judge family, the guard fix, and the power-replication arms. Second, degenerate agreement regimes exist where binned ECE is unidentifiable, which is why this paper pre-commits flip adjudication before any calibration claim. A standard replacement flow for binned ECE under degenerate distributions remains, to our knowledge, without an agreed procedure; that claim is inconclusive and hedged pending a final pre-submission literature re-screen.

Matchers

Exact matching applies the vendored normalizer only (strip, lowercase, subscript translation, punctuation removal) and requires normalized equality. Tolerant matching uses the vendored label_match_v3 <code>tolerant_match</code>: normalization, then unit-suffix stripping (``年'', ``元'', ``米'', ``小时'', ``公里'', ``秒'', ``约'', ``的''), synonym mapping (``不会''/``不是'' to ``否''; ``会''/``yes'' to ``是''; English variants), mixed Chinese-English handling, leading negation/affirmation prefixes for ``否''/``是'', substring matching restricted to non-numeric gold strings of length at least 2, and numeric equivalence (exact within 1e-6 or relative tolerance 0.5%). Empty answers are always incorrect. Version disclosure: the older <code>verify_labels.py</code> embeds a narrower tolerant subset (10 flips on 60 rows); the authoritative matcher here is label_match_v3 plus the third adjudication round (16 flips on 60 rows). Gate reports disclose this difference.

Outcomes and adjudication

On identical rows we report accuracy, 10-bin ECE (ece10), flip count, and flip rate (flips divided by n). Formal definitions: for $B=10$ bins partitioning $[0,1]$, $\mathrm{ECE} = \sum_{b=1}^{B} (n_b/n)\,|\mathrm{acc}_b - \mathrm{conf}_b|$ with $n_b$ rows, empirical accuracy $\mathrm{acc}_b$, and mean confidence $\mathrm{conf}_b$ in bin $b$. Discordant pairs are reported as counts: $b$ exact-wrong/guarded-right and $c$ exact-right/guarded-wrong rows. No paired test is run: the guarded matcher nests the exact matcher (exact accept implies guarded accept on non-degenerate rows; cf. code, scripts/label_match_v33.py), so $c=0$ by construction and any McNemar p-value would be a definitional tautology ($p=2/2^b$). Layers with $b+c=0$ are reported as no discordants. Binning disclosure: all ECE values in the main table use one equal-width 10-bin convention (bin $i$ is $(i/10,(i+1)/10]$ with $0$ in bin $0$, the Guo-style upper-edge-inclusive rule; scripts/recompute_ece_unified.py, results/ece_unified.json; fourteen strata are housed there, and the hetero-family ECE pair (0.5143/0.381) is housed in results/r18_heteromodel.json and results/r18_heteromodel_adjudication.json under the same ece_equal_width function). Earlier strata were sealed under two other conventions: equal-frequency bins on the prior strata (analysis_d2.py) and a hand-rolled floor-bin equal-width variant on five live strata (scripts/analyze_new60.py and its copies), whose sealed numbers are reproduced by a quirk-faithful replica (a <code>bins.index</code> aliasing quirk in the legacy function, documented in the recomputation script) and retained in the appendix for audit. The old ``0.1692 coincidence'' note is withdrawn: 0.1692 was the equal-frequency computation on guarded labels all along (results/guard_v31.json and results/tempscale.json share function and labels), not a cross-convention coincidence; under the unified convention the cloud-new guarded ECE is 0.0558. The flip set (exact outcome differing from tolerant outcome) must be adjudicated row by row under a semantic-equivalence standard (does the prediction express the correct answer in gold, not string equality) before entering calibration analysis. The 16 prior flips were first adjudicated in-session and then re-adjudicated by a heterogeneous judge on the cloud host (qwen2.5:7b, temperature 0, evidence/cloud_judge_16.json): 16 of 16 judged correct for the tolerant matcher, in full agreement with the in-session ruling. The authoring model is muse-spark, so the judge is heterogeneous with respect to authorship. No external gold judge set exists; judge FNR/FPR are characterized on 220 sealed pairs plus 60 adversarial negatives (author-labeled formula-confusable and CJK-fragment items): all five judge configurations stay below the enable bar (results/judge_calib_report.json; local gemma3:4b posts FPR 0.9 on negatives and is disqualified as a correctness judge, local qwen2:7b posts FPR 0.35, deepseek-r1:7b posts FPR 0.05 but keeps a pilot false positive; no judge enabled; author grades carry measured reliability (Cohen $\kappa=0.91$ vs a blinded second rater on a 30-item subset, sole divergence H64 adjudicated against the author; results/rater2_kappa.json)). The 5 cloud-new flips were adjudicated by the same heterogeneous judge (evidence/cloud_judge_new60.json): 4 of 5 judged correct (numeric/unit/synonymy) and 1 judged incorrect (cloud-new-42, ``O'' vs ``O2'': atomic oxygen is not molecular oxygen, a substring over-match on a short chemical formula). A confirmation re-run of all 21 prior flips on the cloud host with the identical prompt reproduced 16 of 16 and 4 of 5 with zero deviation (evidence/cloud_judge_confirm2.json); a shortened prompt variant once flipped pilot-30, and the original prompt re-tested 3 of 3 CORRECT, so the episode is prompt sensitivity rather than model instability. The 5 local-new flips were adjudicated in-session under the same standard (evidence/local_adjudication.json): 5 of 5 correct (same-line confidence suffix, numeric tolerance, book-title brackets, unit suffix, full-name containment). The 4 local-big flips were adjudicated in-session under the same standard (evidence/big_adjudication.json): 4 of 4 correct (trailing-decimal numeric equivalence). The English stratum triggered no adjudication (0 flips). A second-judge attempt for local-new with qwen3:4b failed on single-word instruction compliance (verdicts echoed the instructions and were discarded, never recorded as evidence); single-rater adjudication for the extension strata is disclosed as a limitation. A cloud test-retest of the same 60 cloud-new questions on the same model, prompt, and temperature (scripts/cloud_retest60.py, evidence/cloud_retest60_raw.json, results/retest60.json) reaches exact-string agreement 0.9833 on 60 pairs and guarded agreement 1.0, with identical raw-basis flip sets (5 and 5, including the documented O2 mis-accept; guarded basis 4 and 4) and mean absolute confidence drift 0.0107; the single differing string preserves the flip state (exact-wrong and guarded-right in both runs), so the cloud-new adjudication carries over, and the paired McNemar comparison is degenerate (b=0, c=0, p=1.0, disclosed). A blinded gold set (GOLD-25: a census of all 14 flips carrying stems plus a seeded sample of 11 adjudication-core flips without archived stems, order shuffled, verdicts sealed in evidence/gold_key.json) was graded blindly item by item by the author without access to guarded outcomes (gold_grading_pack.csv, grades_gold25_user.csv): the pre-registered decode reports 25 of 25 under its all-guarded-correct assumption (scripts/analyze_gold.py, results/gold_agreement.json), but that assumption fails on G06 (trace cloud-new-42, guarded-incorrect), so agreement against guarded outcomes is 24 of 25 (agreement 0.96, Wilson 95% CI 0.805 to 0.993; 13 of 14 with stems, 11 of 11 stemless; scripts/recompute_gold_guarded.py, results/gold_guarded_agreement.json). Exact-match non-flip rows were excluded from the gold set by design (their answers equal the reference verbatim, hence zero information on overturn quality), and the set is flip-enriched rather than a random sample, so the 24 of 25 bounds overturn quality on flip strata only, with the single divergence disclosed above; GOLD-100 is stratified by design (not a random sample), contributes 9 guard false negatives outside the flip set (plus the H64 author slip) plus 1 sampled gold whose reference was factually wrong at sampling time and corrected post-hoc (H09), and its echo-exclusion divergences (H47/H85) cut against the sealed exclusion. A 100-item stratified extension (GOLD-100: all 7 exclusions plus 55 confirmed flips plus 38 non-flip rows including both-wrong negatives, blinded with the key sealed) was graded blindly by the author (gold100_grading_pack.csv, grades_gold100_user.csv, GOLD100_README.md, evidence/gold100_key.json, decode scripts/decode_gold100.py; results/gold100_agreement.json); see the GOLD-100 paragraph below for the decoded numbers. Adjudication accounting convention (used identically in the Abstract, Contributions, and every stratum paragraph): judged counts every row submitted to any judge pass, including 4 non-flip probe rows carried as controls (R18 lr18-42 plus hetero-family gr18-41/42/45, all judge-rejected and guard-rejected under v3.3); confirmed counts rows accepted correct on content in the final ruling. Canonical rollup: 106 judged (102 flip rows plus 4 probes), 99 confirmed (scripts/seal_R25_numbers.py, results/adjudication_recount_R25.json).

A component ablation (scripts/ablation.py, results/ablation.json) attributes each of the 16 prior flips to the earliest-firing v3 rule: synonymy 7, numeric tolerance 4, unit suffix 3, substring 1, negation prefix 1.

A two-rule substring guard (v3.3, scripts/label_match_v33.py, results/guard_v33.json; v3.1 formula rule plus the R22 single-CJK-character rule) refuses substring acceptance exactly when both normed sides match <code>\^{</code>[a-z0-9]\{1,4\}\$} while differing with minimum length at most 2 (short-ASCII formula-confusable pairs such as ``O'' vs ``O2''); such pairs fall through to numeric equivalence, which rejects them. Multi-character CJK name pairs are non-ASCII and unaffected by either rule; single-character CJK sides are blocked by the v3.3 rule and fall through to numeric equivalence, which rejects them. Circularity disclosure: the guard was constructed after seeing the 120-row adjudication, so its exact reproduction of those labels is in-sample by construction; its out-of-sample support comes from the fresh-question power-replication arms (10 of 10 flips confirmed correct, zero guard-triggered disputes) and the 470-row zero-difference regression, not from the fit itself; a synthetic targeting test now measures the boundary (see evidence pointers): formula recall and precision 12 of 12, accept specificity 12 of 12, CJK-fragment catch 8 of 8 under the v3.3 patch (8 of 8 missed before it).

A v3.2 revision (scripts/label_match_v32.py, results/guard_v32.json) adds a single raw-fallback inside the empty-answer guard: when both raw answers are non-empty but both normalize to empty, the decision falls back to stripped-raw equality. The 470-row regression against v3.1 shows zero decision differences with zero unexpected outcomes, so v3.1 remains the reported basis and v3.2 is the current vendored implementation with identical decisions on all existing evidence.

A temperature-scaling comparison (scripts/tempscale.py, results/tempscale.json) fits a scalar temperature per stratum on adjudicated labels (confidence clipping 1e-6, temperature bounds 0.05 to 20) and reports NLL plus equal-frequency 10-bin ECE at temperature 1 versus the fitted temperature.

Discussion

What the numbers license: matcher choice moves measured accuracy on adjudicated rows, with the mechanism attributed (synonymy 7, numeric tolerance 4, unit suffix 3, substring 1, negation prefix 1 on the 16 prior flips); a one-rule guard reproduces the 120-row adjudication exactly in-sample (one fix, zero regressions), the same-question cross-model stratum replicates the flip pattern descriptively (5 of 60, 0.0833), a same-question same-model retest reproduces the flip set exactly (guarded agreement 1.0 on 60 pairs), a blinded 25-item gold set grades 24 of 25 against guarded outcomes (Wilson 95% CI 0.805 to 0.993) across five flip-carrying strata, the single divergence being the documented false-positive case, a second question family bounds the phenomenon (0 of 40 on pure numerics), the pre-specified pool shows 9 b-cell flips of 220 (rate 0.0409, descriptive), and a new cross-domain domain shows an instruction-format regime contrast on identical questions (tight 1 of 60 versus loose 20 of 60; 21 of 21 confirmed on content by a second heterogeneous judge family), and a pre-registered loose replication on 60 fresh questions confirms the regime direction descriptively (raw 26 of 60, adjudicated 24 of 60; 24 of 26 confirmed on content), and a hetero-family replication (gemma3:4b, same 60 questions) contributes 8 adjudicated flips of 60 (raw 8; 8 of 8 confirmed on content), so all four former false-positive exemplars are now guard-caught (G06 formula by the v3.1 rule; lr18-42 plus three hetero-family single-character fragments by the v3.3 rule), the phenomenon is not single-model (across qwen, deepseek-judge, and gemma families) but is question-type-dependent and prompt-regime-dependent. ECE moves track the accuracy shift (near-degenerate confidences) and no independent calibration-improvement claim is made. The counted descriptive set is: pilot, pre-specified pool, cross-domain loose, extended holdout, and extended local-new b-cell readings, plus the PowLocalNew single arm (b=7, c=0, no power conclusion) and the raw-basis cloud-new reading (6 of 217), all reported without tests. What they do not license: (i) paired significance in any stratum (no test is run under nesting; ceilings 50/108/217/217/720 are sensitivity context, of which holdout-108, cloud-new-217, and local-new-217 are now met with 108, 217, and 217 rows (pilot-50 remains a 30-row stratum plus a 20-row descriptive cross-model extension, cf. Extended strata); math has no discordant pairs); (ii) cross-model, cross-type, or cross-scale generalization beyond the studied strata (five small open-source-grade models, four question sets; no 70B-plus or closed model tested; all but the pool are post-registration extensions); (iii) post-hoc recalibration as a consistent ECE fix here (temperature scaling improves NLL everywhere but moves unified ECE inconsistently, including at n=157; isotonic regression fails 4 of 5 held-out ECE tests with one positive at PowLocalNew, small-sample overfit elsewhere); (iv) a magnitude claim on the pre-specified pool (0.0409 misses the 5% descriptive bar).

Directional asymmetry and the boundary family. Across all formal strata, discordant cells are b-only (pilot 11/0; holdout-main 5/0; cloud-new guarded 4/0; local-new 5/0; local-big 4/0; R18-loose-rep adjudicated 24/0; hetero-family adjudicated 8/0; pre-specified pool 9/0), while zero-discordant layers (English 0 of 30, math 0 of 40, retest 0 of 60) bound the phenomenon to answer styles carrying units, synonyms, or name variants. This one-sidedness is by construction, not an empirical discovery: the guarded matcher nests the exact matcher, so exact accept implies guarded accept on non-degenerate rows (cf. code, scripts/label_match_v33.py). The only c-channel is degenerate: a single code-format probe item under v3.1 (``//'' vs ``//'', both sides normalizing to empty and hitting the empty-answer guard), closed by the v3.2 raw-fallback with zero decision changes on 470 rows. The probe is feasibility-grade (n=12, distinct from the 30-row pilot stratum), an existence proof only. The cross-domain pair (tight 1 of 60, loose 20 of 60; b=21, c=0 combined as a descriptive sum on identical questions) shows the asymmetry beyond the original domain while keeping c at zero, and identifies instruction format as a regime contrast for the paraphrase surface rather than a universal switch. The power-replication arms (10 tight flips, 7 loose-regime flips, all b-cell) and the extended strata (b=11/7/5/12, c=0 throughout) keep c at zero across 1062 pairs plus 412 regime-contrast rows; the loose-regime non-replication of the prompt-regime contrast bounds the regime-contrast claim to question styles with a paraphrase surface.

Accuracy-regime distinction. The flip phenomenon reads differently across accuracy regimes. On the high-accuracy strata (exact accuracy 0.6333 pilot and holdout-main, 0.80 cloud-new and local-new, 0.925 local-big) at most 8 rows per stratum remain wrong after adjudicated tolerant matching (pilot 0, holdout-main 6, cloud-new 8, local-new 7, local-big 8), so flips there are matcher strictness on otherwise-correct output. On the R18 loose-replication (exact 0.25) and hetero-family (exact 0.35) strata, 21 of 60 and 31 of 60 rows remain wrong even after adjudicated tolerant matching, so the high flip counts there (24 and 8 adjudicated) partly reflect model output failure, not matcher strictness alone; the cross-domain loose layer sits between (exact 0.4167, 15 of 60 still wrong after guarded adjudication). Guarded-accuracy claims are therefore reported per stratum, and no pooled flip rate is interpreted as a pure matcher effect.
